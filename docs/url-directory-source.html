URL Directory

Every address on the platform, who it is for, and what it does  ·  9 October 2026

Use https://www.tryjalla.com — with the www. The bare tryjalla.com answers a redirect to the www host, so both appear to work. But every redirect is one more step that can drop a code out of a sign-up or invitation link, so the www form is the one to share.

A contractor onboards at /contractor-signup and a verifier at /verifier-signup — one page each. Neither uses the client sign-up page. A contractor can open their page directly; a verifier needs the link we email them.

Contractors

Both kinds of contractor onboard at the same URL: https://www.tryjalla.com/contractor-signup. You can send a partner straight there — nothing to email first, no code to read out. What differs is only how they came to us.

An applicant A partner
How they reach us Fills in the form at /contractor-apply We already know them; they bring their own clients
What we do Interview, then approve at /admin/applications Interview

Where they sign up /contractor-signup /contractor-signup
Listed in the directory clients browse Yes — published when you approve them No — you publish them from /admin/contractors if you want them listed

An approved applicant's email link carries a one-time code — /contractor-signup?t=…. It greets them by name and ties the account to their application and the directory listing you published. They never see or type it.

A partner opens the same page with nothing after it and fills the form in. Signing up this way grants no access to any project — that comes only from being brought onto one — and does not put them in the directory clients browse, which only you can publish to. So the page is safe to hand out: anyone who signs up uninvited sees an empty workspace and nothing else.

Verifiers

Verifiers have no public application page, by design. A verifier's sign-off is what releases a client's stage payment, so the role is never self-served — we interview the engineer first, then invite them.

  1. We interview them. No URL; this happens off the platform.
  2. We invite them from https://www.tryjalla.com/admin/verifiers, using the "Invite a verifier" panel at the top. Enter their name and email.
  3. They onboard at https://www.tryjalla.com/verifier-signup — the link in that invitation email. The heading and the button both say "Sign up as a verifier". They set their own password; staff never choose one for them.

Re-sending an invitation to the same address re-sends the link they already have, rather than creating a second live one. Opening /verifier-signup without a valid invitation shows no form at all.

Admin and staff

Staff sign in at https://www.tryjalla.com/admin/login. There is no admin sign-up page and no Google sign-in there — both deliberately, since either would be a way to grant yourself staff access. An admin account is created by another admin.

Everything below needs an admin role. Opening one of them while signed out sends you to the staff sign-in and brings you back afterwards; opening one while signed in as a client or contractor sends you to your own dashboard. Nobody sees a half-loaded admin page.

Everything above is live confirmed 9 Oct

Migrations 104, 105 and 106 were applied to production on 9 October 2026, and each one was confirmed against the live database. Every URL in this document works today, including /work/payouts, /verifier-signup and the partner route into /contractor-signup.

The checks also confirmed the access rules hold in production: the two pages that greet someone before they have an account can read just that one invitation, while claiming an account, issuing an invitation and granting a role are all refused to an anonymous caller. Invitation tokens are not readable or listable by anyone.

Sidebar items with no page behind them not built

Four admin sidebar entries are deliberate placeholders — they appear in the navigation but have no screen yet. Worth knowing before anyone clicks one in a demo.

One thing that is still manual

A verifier's credentials — their discipline, registration body and city — have no screen yet. Inviting them creates the account and grants the role correctly; those three fields stay blank in the directory until we build a form for them.
