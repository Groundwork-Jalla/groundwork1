Before you start

You need two things: an admin account on Groundwork, and the client's details already collected — their full name, an email address they can actually open, and ideally a phone number. The email matters more than anything else on the form: it becomes their username, and a code is sent to it the first time they sign in. An address they cannot open is an account they cannot enter.

The order matters

The account comes first, the project second. A project has to belong to somebody, so there is nothing to attach a build to until the client exists. If you are already on the project screen and the client is not in the list, there is a link there that takes you back to create them — Step 6 covers it.

Part one

Creating the client's account

Roughly two minutes. It ends with a username and a password written on your screen, which you read out or send to the client.

01

Sign in to the admin area

Go to www.tryjalla.com/admin/login

This is a separate door from the one clients use. There is no “sign up” here and no Google button — deliberately: an admin account is only ever created by another admin.

Sign in with your own email and password. If two-factor authentication is switched on for your account, you will be asked for a code before you get in. You land on the Overview page, with the full admin menu down the left.

02

Open the client list

Go to www.tryjalla.com/admin/users

In the left-hand menu, under People, click Users. This is everyone who has an account on Groundwork — clients, contractors and staff alike.

Before creating anyone, search for them first. The search box takes a name or an email address. If they are already here, do not create a second account; open the existing one. Creating a duplicate splits their projects across two logins and neither one shows the whole picture.

When you are sure they are new, click Create a client account, top right.

03

Fill in what you collected

You are on www.tryjalla.com/admin/users/new
FieldWhat to enter
Full nameAs they would write it themselves. It appears on their dashboard and on anything Groundwork sends them.
Email addressTheir username, and where the sign-in code goes. Check it twice — a typo here means an account nobody can enter.
PhoneOptional. Useful for reaching them; not used to sign in.
CountryCameroon unless you know otherwise.
Language for emailsEnglish or French. Everything Groundwork sends them follows this. They can change it later themselves.

Click Create account. Nothing is emailed to the client — you hand the details over yourself.

04

Hand over the two credentials

The screen changes to show two things, with a copy button beside each:

  • Username — the email address you just entered.
  • Temporary password — generated for you, in the shape Kf7m-Rq3x-Wn9p-Ht4y. Deliberately made of characters that survive being read aloud: no zero against letter O, no one against letter l.
This password is shown once and is stored nowhere

Leave the page and it is gone for good — not recoverable by you, by Favour, or from any record. Copy it out now: read it to the client on a call, or put it in a message you delete afterwards.

If it is lost anyway, do not create a second account. Send the client to www.tryjalla.com/auth/reset-password and have them use Forgot password? — that works, because the email address is already confirmed.

Part two

Creating the project for them

Ten screens of build questions, then the budget. The project belongs to the client from the moment it is created — it appears on their dashboard, not yours — and Groundwork records that you set it up.

05

Start a project for that client

Go to www.tryjalla.com/admin/projects

In the left menu, under Work, click Projects, then Create for a client, top right.

If you came straight from the handover screen in Step 4, you have skipped this — you are already at the next step with the client chosen.

06

Choose whose project it is

You are on www.tryjalla.com/admin/projects/new

Search by name or email, then click the client. Staff accounts are left out of this list on purpose — you are picking the person who will own the build.

If they are not there, they have no account yet. The line at the bottom — “Client not on Groundwork yet? Create their account first” — takes you back to Part 1, and returns you here with them already selected.

Going straight to one client

Each client has a direct address: www.tryjalla.com/admin/projects/new?for=<client id>. That is exactly what the Create their project button uses. You never have to construct it by hand, but if you have that link it skips the search.

07

Work through the build questions

This is the same questionnaire a client would fill in themselves, with one difference you can see at the top of every screen: an amber line reading Creating for [name] · Jalla Management. As long as that line is there, everything you type is being recorded against their project, not yours.

The counter top right reads 1 / 11 through 11 / 11. The plan-choice screen (number 10) is skipped automatically — an admin-created project is Jalla Management by definition, so there is nothing to choose.

ScreenWhat it asks
1 · LocationCountry. Cameroon is offered first.
2 · Project TypeWhat kind of build this is.
3 · BuildingThe building type.
4 · FloorsGround floor only, or how many storeys above it.
5 · RoomsRoom composition, one floor at a time. You must put at least one room on each floor before it will let you move to the next — this is what stops an upper floor being left empty by accident and under-pricing the build.
6 · QuartersWhether there is a boys' quarters.
7 · RoofRoof type.
8 · DetailsProject name, ground-floor footprint in m², and the city. The city matters: rates differ by location and the estimate is adjusted for it.
9 · SummaryEverything so far, with the estimated budget and predicted build timeline. Read this back before continuing.
11 · ConfirmThe budget. Covered in the next step.

Back and Continue move between screens, and the numbered dots at the top jump to any screen you have already completed. Nothing is saved to the client's account until the last step, so leaving half-way loses the answers — but creates nothing wrong.

08

Confirm the budget and attach the BoQ

The last screen shows Groundwork's own estimate and a field holding the same figure, labelled Your final budget. Overwrite it with the real contract figure whenever you have one. Every stage payment is derived from whatever number is in that field, so it should be the number the build is actually being run on, not the estimate.

Underneath is Upload your contractor's quote or bill of quantities — an Excel BoQ is exactly what it is for. It accepts PDF, Word, Excel (.xls / .xlsx), CSV, OpenDocument spreadsheets and images, up to 20 MB, and files it into that project's Documents tab under Bill of Quantities, stored privately — never on a public link.

Click Confirm budget & create project. The project is created, its first stage opens, and you are returned to /admin/projects, where it now appears.

If the file is refused

You are told immediately, before the project is created, and nothing else is lost — pick a different file and carry on. If the build is created but the attachment fails at the last moment, the project page says so and the file can be added afterwards from the project's Documents tab.

Part three

What happens on the client's side

Worth knowing, because they will ring you about it.

09

Their first sign-in

Send them to www.tryjalla.com/auth/login

Three things happen, in this order:

  1. They sign in with the email and temporary password you gave them.
  2. A six-digit code is emailed to that address. Entering it proves the inbox is theirs. This is why the email address had to be one they can open.
  3. They are asked to choose a password of their own before they can see anything else. The temporary password stops working at that moment — so the one you knew is no longer a way into their account.

After that they land on their dashboard, with the project you built already there.

10

If something goes wrong

What you hearWhat to do
“I've lost the password you gave me” Send them to /auth/reset-password and have them use Forgot password?. Do not create a second account.
“No code arrived” Have them check spam first. There is a resend link on the code screen. If it still does not arrive, the address is probably wrong — check it on /admin/users.
“That email already exists” Someone already has an account on it. Find them on /admin/users and use that account. Groundwork refuses to issue a fresh password for an address somebody already owns.
They never signed in /admin/users shows Awaiting first sign-in under their name for exactly this. It clears itself the moment they choose their own password.
Wrong name or email on the account The client can change both themselves under Settings → Account. An email change is confirmed by link before it takes effect.
The budget figure was wrong It can be revised afterwards from the project's Costing tab. Stage payments recalculate from the new figure.
