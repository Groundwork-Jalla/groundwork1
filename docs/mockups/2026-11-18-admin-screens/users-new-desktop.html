What are they?

HomeownerA Jalla Management homeowner. They get a managed plan and you create their project next.
ContractorA construction partner. They see a project only after they are invited to it.
VerifierAn engineer who signs off stages. They see a stage only after they are assigned to verify it.

Admins can change this later under Users → Change user role. Role changes are recorded in the audit log.

An account with that email already exists. Find them on the users page instead — a new password cannot be issued for an account someone already owns.

Create accountNo email is sent. You hand over the details yourself.

What happens at their first sign-in

  1. 1They sign in at Groundwork with the username and temporary password you are given next.
  2. 2A six-digit code is sent to their email. Entering it proves the inbox is theirs.
  3. 3They are asked to choose a password of their own before seeing anything else. The temporary one stops working at that moment.

Until then the account shows Awaiting first sign-in in Users.
