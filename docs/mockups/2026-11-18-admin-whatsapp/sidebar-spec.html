The admin sidebar, in dropdowns

All 38 items stay, with the same labels, order and destinations as today (src/components/shell/nav-config.ts). They now sit in dropdowns, so the sidebar fits the screen without scrolling.

How it opens: the group holding the page you are on opens by itself, so on WhatsApp, Communication is open and WhatsApp is lit. Click any other group to open it. Overview and Action Center stay at the top, and Support and Analytics, one item each, stay as plain links.

One fix: an item is lit only when its whole address matches, including ?channel=. Today Inbox, WhatsApp, Jalla Messages, Email and Calls all light up at once.
